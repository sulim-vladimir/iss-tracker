How to
  1. Camera with its lens in the 1.25" holder, screw snug. A white card in the tube opposite the focuser, the tube aimed at a bright wall or the daytime sky (never the Sun). A white paper collar round the lens makes its reflection easy to see.
  2. Focus the lens on the secondary's edge, then back it off a little - between the secondary and the donut. Blur is fine: the centre of a blurred circle is still exact.
  3. Pick each circle: its button, then 3-6 clicks spread round its edge, then Done.
  4. Focuser axis: 1st frame, turn the camera 180° in the holder, 2nd frame.
  5. Adjust in order: 1 (rarely needed), 2 with the secondary's screws, 3 with the primary's. Then check 2 and 3 once more.
